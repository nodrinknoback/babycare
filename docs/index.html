This policy applies to the BabyCare Android app (com.zero.babycare). Developer: copydog. Privacy contact: nodrinknoback@gmail.com.

1. Data processed on your device and its purpose
BabyCare helps parents and guardians record a baby’s daily life. Names, sex, dates of birth, feeding, sleep, diaper, temperature, medicine, vaccination, growth, activity and note data that you enter are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. They support record management, local statistics, growth charts, feeding and sleep estimates, and reminders. Settings and timer state are also stored locally. Only enter information you are authorized to manage.

2. Collection, sharing and third parties
The current version needs no account. It does not upload care records to the developer’s servers, sell them or share them with advertisers. It includes no advertising, behavioral analytics or remote crash reporting services. Statistics and predictions run on your device. Components such as Room and MMKV process data locally. The current app does not request Internet access permission.

3. Permissions, notifications and backups
Notification permission is used only for local care reminders you enable. You can disable reminders in the app or system settings. Reminder text may appear on the lock screen; adjust system notification privacy settings as needed. Import and export use the system file picker to access files you select, without access to all device storage. Exported backups contain the baby profiles and care records you export and are not encrypted. Store them in a trusted location. If you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the file under its own privacy policy. Android automatic backup is disabled for the app.

4. Retention, security and deletion
Data stays on your device until you delete records or a baby profile, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Deleting a baby also deletes associated records. Exported files remain separately and must be deleted from their storage location. The app relies on Android app isolation and device security; it does not provide separate database or backup encryption. The developer has no copy of your local records and cannot restore or remotely delete them for you. Protect your device and backups.

5. Children’s information and health notice
The app is for adult caregivers, not for independent use by children. Parents or guardians manage the baby’s information. BabyCare is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Growth references, predictions and reminders help organize records and do not replace professional medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

6. Contact and policy changes
For privacy questions or deletion of support emails you voluntarily send, contact nodrinknoback@gmail.com. Support emails and voluntarily supplied information are used only to handle your request and deleted when the request is resolved and retention is no longer necessary. Email providers process messages under their own policies. Do not send unnecessary baby health details. The public policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and access logs when you visit it; see the GitHub Privacy Statement (GitHub Privacy Statement). Changes to this policy will be dated in the app and on the public page.
